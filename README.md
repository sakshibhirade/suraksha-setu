🛡️ Suraksha Setu
A Smarter Way to Report, Understand and Stay Aware of Cyber Threats

Suraksha Setu is a mobile-first cybersecurity platform designed to make digital safety more accessible. It focuses on helping users report cybersecurity incidents, understand potential online threats, and improve their awareness of common cyber risks.

As digital services become part of our everyday lives, cyber threats such as phishing, suspicious links, online scams and digital fraud are becoming increasingly difficult for ordinary users to identify.

Suraksha Setu aims to bridge this gap by bringing incident reporting, threat analysis and cybersecurity awareness together in one mobile application.

---

🎯 The Problem We're Addressing

Many people experience cyber incidents but do not know how to identify them, report them or take appropriate action.

Some of the common challenges include:

- Difficulty recognizing suspicious online activities.
- Lack of awareness about phishing, scams and other cyber threats.
- Limited understanding of what to do after encountering a cyber incident.
- Lack of accessible platforms that encourage users to report suspicious activities.

Suraksha Setu is designed to make these processes more approachable through a simple, mobile-first experience.

💡 Our Solution

Suraksha Setu brings together three important aspects of cybersecurity:

1. Incident Reporting: A platform focused on making cybersecurity incident reporting more accessible.
2. Threat Analysis: A heuristic-based scanner designed to help users examine potential online threats.
3. Cybersecurity Awareness: An awareness-focused approach to help users understand digital risks and practice safer online behaviour.

Our goal is not just to help users respond to cyber threats, but also to help them become more informed and cautious digital citizens.

✨ Key Capabilities

1. Cybersecurity Incident Reporting
Provides a dedicated approach for users to report cybersecurity-related incidents and suspicious activities.

2. Threat Heuristic Scanner
Uses heuristic-based analysis to examine potential threats and help users recognize suspicious digital content.

3. Cybersecurity Awareness
Focuses on improving users' understanding of common cyber threats and safer digital practices.

4. AI Integration
Integrates Google's Gemini API for AI-powered capabilities within the application.

*Note: Automated analysis is intended to support user awareness and should not be treated as a guarantee that content is safe or malicious.*
